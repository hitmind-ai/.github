## HitMind

AI SEO tooling. Live today at [hitmind.ai](https://hitmind.ai), free and without signup:

- **[SEO audit](https://hitmind.ai/audit/)** — five measured dimensions (performance, SEO, mobile, security, international fit). Every report gets a permanent link, and the snapshot never changes, so you can re-run it later and compare.
- **[Orphan page checker](https://hitmind.ai/tools/orphan-page-checker/)** — crawls a site from its homepage and lists the sitemap pages that nothing links to.
- **[Smaller tools](https://hitmind.ai/tools)** — meta description generator, SERP title pixel checker, and a robots.txt generator that also evaluates a pasted file the way a crawler would.

Autopilot publishing — drafting and publishing the content an audit calls for, then reporting results from the site's own Google Search Console — is in development and not yet live.

Our crawler identifies itself as `HitMindBot`. What it fetches and how to control it: [hitmind.ai/bot](https://hitmind.ai/bot).
